# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary — fans, parents, and the local community** browsing on mobile during or after match day. They want to find current news, upcoming fixtures, and team rosters quickly. Most visits are short and goal-directed (check the score, read a report, find the venue).

**Secondary — players and trainers** who use the protected dashboard to manage training plans, player profiles, and team assignments. Their context is desktop or tablet, workflow-oriented, time-pressured before or after training sessions.

**Tertiary — prospective members and sponsors** who arrive from a search or recommendation and need to understand who the club is and how to get involved.

## Product Purpose

TG MIPA Landshut Handball's official digital home. It makes the club legible, accessible, and credible to fans, members, and partners — and provides an internal tool so trainers can manage their squads without a third-party platform.

Success looks like: a fan can find today's result in under 10 seconds on mobile; a prospective sponsor sees a club that takes itself seriously; a trainer can upload a training plan and assign it to their players without needing IT support.

## Brand Personality

Bold · Athletic · Competitive

The club runs on genuine local passion — not corporate polish. The design should feel like a real sports organisation that means business: high-contrast, direct, no fluff. Energy comes from the sport itself, not from stock-photo production values.

## Anti-references

- **Generic sports SaaS / template aesthetics**: no blue-gradient hero + metric cards, no Wix Sports / GoSportsite look. No stock-photo athletes against gradient backgrounds.
- **AI-default warm neutrals**: no cream/sand/parchment body backgrounds. The brand colors (red #da251c, blue #004f9f) carry the identity — not a warm neutral that could be any club.
- **Eyebrow-every-section scaffolding**: no tiny ALL-CAPS kickers above every heading. One deliberate branded pattern is fine; reflexive scaffolding is not.
- **Glassmorphism, gradient text, side-stripe cards**: all banned per design system rules.

## Design Principles

1. **Sport first** — every layout decision serves the athlete and the result, not decoration. Information hierarchy is clear at a glance.
2. **Club colors as the brand** — red and blue are not accents; they carry the identity. Use them with commitment, not as highlights on a neutral field.
3. **Mobile match-day** — the primary context is a fan checking their phone at the pitch. Speed, contrast, and thumb-reachable targets are non-negotiable.
4. **Local, not corporate** — the site should feel like it belongs to a real community club in Landshut. Authentic, direct, a little rough around the edges where that's honest.
5. **Earn every visual element** — nothing decorative for its own sake. If an animation, shadow, or icon can be removed without losing meaning, remove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum across all public pages. Existing implementation includes: keyboard-navigable header with aria labels, skip-to-content link, `:focus-visible` ring in club red, meaningful alt text on all images. Maintain and extend these patterns. `prefers-reduced-motion` must have a static fallback for every animation.
